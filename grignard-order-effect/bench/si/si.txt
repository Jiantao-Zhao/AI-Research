The M06-2X xc functional is the best in reproducing CC data, in particular, showing a discrepancy not
higher than 1.2 kcal mol-1 in the dissociation energy. The GGA PBE functional, used in the AIMD runs
thanks to its computational efficiency, shows a reasonable performance, even though it tends to
underestimate homolytic dissociation energies by about 4 kcal mol-1.

is 137 ± 9o.
